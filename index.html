ONLINE MULTIPLAYER GUIDE

How to Play Over the Internet

Want to play with someone in another room, house, or country? Here are the 3 easiest ways:

⚡ Method 1: Instant Free Tunnel (Zero Setup)

Open a terminal in your project directory and run:

npx localtunnel --port 3000

It gives you a public link (e.g., https://wild-dare.loca.lt). Anyone anywhere in the world on a phone or laptop can open that link!

📶 Method 2: Same Wi-Fi (Home / School / Room)

If both players are on the same Wi-Fi, the second player can open your local IP:

http://192.168.1.65:3000

Just type this IP address into the browser of their phone or computer!

☁️ Method 3: 24/7 Free Cloud Hosting (Render / Railway)

Push this repository to GitHub and connect it to Render.com as a free Web Service (node server.js). You'll get a permanent 24/7 web address (e.g., https://truth-dare.onrender.com)!
